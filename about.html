I’m Jonah—a video creator and photographer who’s been making videos since I was a kid. I love finding a new creative project and figuring out how to make it engaging and compelling.

I work across pre-production, production, and post-production—from developing creative briefs and campaign concepts to filming and editing in DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, and CapCut.

Away from the camera, I love fly fishing, mountain biking, and hiking with my wife.
